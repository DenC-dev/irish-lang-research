# Data rules for this project

1. **No data from or about children is collected, stored or published here.** Example sentences are constructed.
2. **No scraping of social platforms.** The only online-language data used is the published, licensed UD TwittIrish treebank, in which usernames, phone numbers and emails were anonymised by its maintainers.
3. **Aggregate output only.** Scripts write counts and rates. They do not write tweet text.
4. **No profiling.** The feature extractor reports on texts. Do not aggregate its output by author, and do not use it to label a person as a learner, native speaker, or member of any group.
5. **Small-community risk.** Irish-language networks are small enough that dialect, school and style can identify a writer after direct identifiers are removed. Treat any future text collection as identifiable by default.
6. **If this becomes a study with pupils:** university ethics approval, guardian consent and child assent, a data protection impact assessment, agreement with the school and its patron body, and a plan to return findings to the school community come before any collection.
